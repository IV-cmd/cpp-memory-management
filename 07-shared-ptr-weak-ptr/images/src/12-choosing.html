How I pick one
Does the lifetime fit a scope?
use a plain automatic object
↓
One clear owner?
std::unique_ptr
↓
Lifetime genuinely shared?
std::shared_ptr
↓
Just using it?
reference, raw pointer or std::span, with a clear lifetime guarantee
↓
Observing a shared object?
std::weak_ptr
Plain new and delete don't appear in this list.
